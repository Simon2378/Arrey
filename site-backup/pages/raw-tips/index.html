We offer two types of RAW tips to choose from!

RAW Perforated Wide Tips

RAW Perfecto Pre-Rolled Cone Tips are the ultimate foundation for building your own soundly constructed, handcrafted cones for a hassle-free smoking experience. Each tip is carefully cut and pre-rolled following the natural grain of the fibers to ensure a perfectly round tip for every cone.
